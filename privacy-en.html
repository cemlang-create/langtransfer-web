2. Hosting

This website is hosted via Netlify (Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, USA). When you access the site, Netlify automatically processes technical access data (server log files), such as IP address, date and time of access, the page accessed, and the browser used. This serves the technical provision and security of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in a secure and functioning operation). Where required, a data processing agreement including EU Standard Contractual Clauses is in place with Netlify for the transfer of data to the USA.

3. Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process the data you provide (name, email address, optionally phone number, message) in order to process and respond to your inquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual inquiry) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry). The form is technically processed via Netlify Forms; the data is deleted once it is no longer required to process your inquiry.

4. Online booking

When you request a ride via our booking form, we process the data you provide (name, contact details, pickup and destination address, travel date, number of passengers, and, if applicable, company details) in order to process and carry out the carriage contract. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual or contractual measure).

We use the following services for the technical implementation:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): storage of booking data (Firestore) and execution of the booking logic (Cloud Functions). A data processing agreement including EU Standard Contractual Clauses is in place with Google.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places API, Distance Matrix API): to validate the addresses entered and calculate the route. The address data you enter is transmitted to Google for this purpose. A data processing agreement including EU Standard Contractual Clauses is also in place here.
  • Email delivery: booking confirmations and status updates are sent via our own IONOS mailbox.
  • Internal calendar: for scheduling and availability planning, your pickup appointment is entered into our internal, non-public Google Calendar.

Your booking data is deleted or anonymized once it is no longer required to carry out the ride, unless statutory retention obligations (in particular under German commercial and tax law, typically 6–10 years for invoice data) require otherwise.

5. Payment processing

After your booking is confirmed, you will receive a payment link. Payment processing (credit card, Klarna, giropay) is carried out by our payment provider Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin, Ireland. The data required for payment (name, email address, payment data, invoice amount) is transmitted directly to Stripe; we ourselves do not receive or store complete payment/card data. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance). Further information on data protection at Stripe is available at stripe.com/en-de/privacy.

6. Fonts

We host the font used (Work Sans) locally on our own server. No connection is made to third-party servers (e.g. Google Fonts), so no personal data is transmitted to third parties when the font is loaded.

7. Cookies & Google Ads

This website embeds the Google tag from Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to measure the reach and success of our advertising campaigns (conversion tracking). This may set cookies and transmit information about your interaction with our website to Google.

These cookies are only set with your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TTDSG). On your first visit, a cookie banner appears with the options "Accept" and "Decline". Without your consent, tracking remains technically disabled (Google Consent Mode, default setting "denied"). You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting this website's cookies stored in your browser – the banner will then appear again.

Further information on data protection at Google is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Beyond the Google tag, this website does not use any further cookies or analytics/tracking services.

Terms of Carriage

1. The carriage contract only comes into effect once we confirm your booking request in writing after review. Until then, your request is non-binding.

2. The stated price is a fixed price based on the route, time and number of passengers given in the request. Changes (e.g. additional stops, waiting time, a different address) may adjust the price.

3. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. For cancellations closer to the pickup time, we charge 50 % of the order total.

4. If a passenger is delayed, the driver waits 5 minutes free of charge; after that, waiting time is billed at €1 per minute.

5. Children require a child seat that meets the statutory requirements; we can provide one on request.

6. The statutory provisions of the German Passenger Transportation Act (PBefG) for private hire vehicles apply.

7. Payment is due after the booking is confirmed, via the payment link included in the confirmation email (credit card, Klarna or giropay, processed by our payment provider Stripe), or alternatively by card directly in the vehicle. Cash payment is not possible.
